Share your audio

Whatever plays on this computer becomes the stream. Everyone who joins hears the same thing at the same moment.

  1. Pick Chrome Tab in the dialog — not a window, not the whole screen. Only a tab can carry sound.
  2. Tick Also share tab audio in the corner of that dialog. It is easy to miss, and without it the stream is silent.
  3. Play the music in that tab. Spotify, YouTube, anything.

To share sound from an app instead of a tab, route it through a loopback input and pick Audio input in the developer panel.
